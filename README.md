# My Current Favorite Anime Series: Jujutsu Kaisen
 
## About
The Story follows Itadori Yuji, who becomes a host of a powerful cursed spirit and joins a school of sorcerers to fight curses.
 
### Why I like it
- Exciting Fighting scenes
- High Quality animation from MAPPA that makes the fighting scenes interesting and fun to watch
- Creative and interesting powers
